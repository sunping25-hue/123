Preview data-use notice

This is a development review version. Use test names, phone numbers and email addresses, and do not submit real personal or sensitive information. Production retention, rights-handling procedures and notifications are still to be confirmed.

1. Scope of this notice

This notice covers contact and demonstration-request forms in the ShinoX V2.0 local preview. Static pages and a preview with the local backend running have different capabilities. Follow the status shown on the form.

2. Information the forms may collect

Depending on the selected form, information may include your name, phone, email, region, enquiry topic, message, demonstration venue and preferred date or time. Enter only the information required for this test.

3. Data storage and notification status

When the local preview backend is running, validated form data is saved to a local database for functional testing. A local record number does not mean sales has been notified, an email has been sent or a demonstration booked. Refer to the result message for the actual outcome.

Without an available backend, the forms cannot save data. To contact ShinoX, call us directly or use the email and WhatsApp links below.

4. Service enquiries and marketing consent are separate

Consent to use data for this enquiry or visit request is not consent to receive marketing. This preview does not send automated marketing emails or add form submissions to a marketing list.

The startup PDF, automated project emails, visit invitations and unsubscribe feature are not active production services. Any future marketing subscription must separately explain its purpose, retention and opt-out process, and obtain independent consent.

5. Demonstration visits require confirmation

A preferred date or time is a request, not real-time availability. The venue, available time and appointment are valid only after confirmation by the ShinoX team.

6. Removing test data and establishing the production policy

Local test data is managed by the execution environment administrator. Records older than 30 days are removed when the local backend starts, not periodically during continuous operation. Remove test records manually after review. Production retention, access permissions, data access/correction/deletion procedures and a full privacy notice must be established before collecting real personal data; this development notice is not a substitute.
