SHINOX / BUSINESS SOLUTIONS

Build your self-service car wash business

Start with a plot of land. Build a self-service car wash business of your own.

Interested in entering the self-service car wash industry, but unsure where to start?

From site conditions, wash bay numbers and vehicle circulation to equipment functions, construction and ongoing operations, every aspect affects development costs, customer experience and long-term operating efficiency.

ShinoX provides comprehensive self-service car wash planning and equipment integration, helping entrepreneurs move from an initial idea to site development and build a business suited to their site conditions and operating needs.

We do more than supply car wash equipment. We focus on how the entire site operates and how its overall layout works.

One-stop integration for a clearer business development plan

01 | Site assessment and feasibility analysis

We assess a suitable number of wash bays and basic development requirements based on land area, site shape, road access, the surrounding environment and the proposed operating model, helping owners establish a planning direction before construction begins.

02 | Wash bay layout and vehicle circulation

We consider vehicle entry and exit, washing space, queuing, equipment room layouts and supporting facilities to create practical site circulation that balances land-use efficiency with a convenient customer experience.

03 | Self-service wash equipment and function integration

We configure high-pressure rinsing, foam cleaning, low-pressure clean water, coating, vacuuming, blow drying and other optional functions according to business positioning and budget requirements to create a self-service wash offering suited to market needs.

04 | Construction coordination and site development

We integrate wash canopies, equipment foundations, water supply and drainage, electrical services, equipment rooms and related works to help owners manage construction, equipment installation and development quality.

05 | Smart payments and operations management

Depending on the equipment solution, we integrate payment methods such as coins, stored value and mobile payments with suitable operations management functions to help owners improve payment convenience and daily management efficiency.

06 | Opening preparation and operational guidance

From equipment testing, operating guidance and opening preparation to ongoing maintenance, we provide relevant technical support and operational advice, giving owners a professional team to consult after opening.

Build more than a car wash: create a business model for long-term operation

Self-service washing lets customers operate the equipment themselves, reducing reliance on on-site service staff and allowing extended operating hours to be planned where site conditions permit.

Business performance depends on more than equipment prices. Location, layout, equipment reliability, investment costs and ongoing management all matter.

ShinoX aims to help every entrepreneur establish a clear development plan and operational direction before committing capital, reducing unnecessary construction rework and waste in equipment configuration.

Your site deserves a plan tailored to its needs.

Whether you already own land, are looking for a suitable site or simply want an introduction to the self-service car wash industry, we welcome a conversation with you.

SHINOX / SIX STAGES

Six-stage franchise & site development process

From the first discussion to a soft opening, review the site, equipment and partnership at each stage. Both parties must confirm assessment arrangements, scope and opening conditions.

STAGE 01 / 06

In-person consultation

Start with your plans, current site conditions and equipment needs, then discuss partnership and site development with our team.

  • Prepare your operating plans and current site information
  • List equipment requirements and partnership questions

STAGE 02 / 06

Site visit & assessment

Review the location, area, access and surroundings. Both parties confirm site-visit arrangements and assessment items.

  • Review site dimensions, lanes and entrances/exits
  • Check utilities, drainage and surrounding constraints

STAGE 03 / 06

Site planning & design

Discuss wash bays, equipment placement, customer flow and maintenance access based on known site conditions and service needs.

  • Discuss bays, lanes and equipment placement
  • Confirm the design scope and outstanding information

STAGE 04 / 06

Agreement signing

Review the partnership scope, equipment and responsibilities at each stage. The agreement is governed by the documents formally signed by both parties.

  • Confirm the partnership scope and equipment
  • Review responsibilities and agreement terms

STAGE 05 / 06

Construction coordination

Coordinate construction, equipment installation and on-site interfaces against the confirmed drawings and agreed scope. Confirm arrangements at each stage.

  • Coordinate construction and equipment delivery
  • Review installation, pipework and on-site interfaces

STAGE 06 / 06

Soft opening

Check operation against the confirmed equipment configuration, record soft-opening observations and review adjustments. Both parties separately confirm the formal opening arrangements.

  • Check equipment operation and the site workflow
  • Record soft-opening observations and follow-up adjustments
