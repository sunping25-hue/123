Touchless Car Wash Machines

What car wash equipment does ShinoX offer?

ShinoX offers two touchless automatic car wash machines, B2 and M9, together with the 9-in-1 car wash control box and other self-service car wash equipment and accessories, configured to suit each site.

What is the difference between B2 and M9?

Both are 360-degree touchless water-jet car wash machines. M9 is the ShinoX high-end model, and B2 comes standard with four 4 kW dryers. The right model for your site is evaluated during the site survey, based on site conditions and needs.

Should I choose a single-arm or dual-arm machine?

B2 and M9 are both available in single-arm and dual-arm configurations. The configuration is evaluated during the site survey, based on each site and its needs.

What is touchless water-jet car washing?

Touchless car washing cleans the vehicle with high-pressure water jets and a cleaning sequence. No brushes touch the paint.

What steps are in the wash process?

Taking B2 as an example, the standard process is undercarriage spray, pre-soak cleaning, high-pressure water-jet rinse, foam waterfall, shine coat, protective coating and drying.

Can I customize the wash programs?

Yes. Operators can set up 12 wash steps, combine them freely, adjust their order and repetitions, and save them as 6 wash-program buttons that drivers select with one touch.

How long does B2 take per car, and how much water does it use?

About 2 minutes 30 seconds and about 100 liters per car, depending on program settings and vehicle size.

What is the warranty?

Equipment comes with a 1-year warranty.

Where can I see the machines in operation?

You can book a visit to one of our demo sites in Tainan, Taiwan: No. 611, Zhongzheng S. Rd., Yongkang Dist.; No. 151, Sec. 2, Yonghua Rd., Anping Dist.; or No. 1-38, Min'an Vil., Jiali Dist.

Self-Service Car Wash

What does the 9-in-1 control box do?

The 9-in-1 control box has 9 function buttons and wet-zone/dry-zone selection. Its screen shows the amount and remaining seconds, and time and pricing are set by the operator. It has dual mechanical coin channels and an unlimited-time mode that can be set as pausable or non-pausable, with its own time and pricing. The housing is stainless steel with powder coating in several colors.

What payment methods are supported?

Coins, stored-value cards, mobile payment and cloud membership points through the 9-in-1 control box.

Can I run a self-service car wash with no prior experience?

Yes. We provide operation training and routine maintenance guidance when the equipment is handed over, so owners new to the business can get started.

What site conditions does a self-service car wash need?

As a general guide, each wash bay needs about 15 ping (roughly 50 m²), 220V three-phase power, mains water, and drainage or wastewater facilities, and the site's permitted use must allow a car wash. We confirm each requirement during the site survey.

I don't have a site yet. Can you help evaluate one?

Yes. We visit the site to assess its conditions, vehicle flow and bay layout, then recommend an equipment plan.

How long does it take from planning to opening?

It depends on site conditions, permit paperwork and on-site works, so there is no fixed timeline. After the site survey, we plan the schedule together based on the actual situation.

Can a self-service car wash run unattended?

Yes. It can be set up for fully self-service, unattended operation, with drivers operating and paying on their own. Owners can schedule site checks, consumable refills and cleaning as needed.

How are consumables handled during the warranty period?

During the warranty period, consumables are supplied by our headquarters. The exact items and supply arrangements are explained before you sign on.

What repair support is available if equipment breaks down?

We offer online troubleshooting, on-site repair visits when needed, and periodic inspections to keep equipment running properly.
