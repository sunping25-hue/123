Japanese Future Wash Concept

Inspired by European car wash systems. Refined through Japanese attention to detail.

ShinoX is developing a new car wash concept that brings together European-style wash bay planning and the careful, user-focused spirit of Japanese car care. The concept aims to connect space design, convenient operation and a distinctive wash experience.

Concept visualization for illustration only; actual equipment and site details may change.

A meeting of two wash cultures

European-style wash sites often emphasize equipment integration, clear traffic flow and practical space planning. Japanese car care places strong attention on the washing experience, cleanliness and details. ShinoX is exploring how these ideas can be combined in a new type of wash bay.

An environment designed around the wash experience

From the positions of washing tools and lighting to vehicle space and customer movement, the concept starts with the actual way people wash their vehicles. The goal is a site that looks modern while remaining intuitive to use.

Three directions for the next generation

01 European-inspired layout

A clear equipment arrangement, orderly circulation and efficient use of space.

02 Japanese-inspired details

Thoughtful lighting, cleanliness and an experience that feels comfortable to use.

03 ShinoX systems integration

Bringing equipment, site planning and the overall user experience together.
