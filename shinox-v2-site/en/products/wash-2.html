07 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose a single-arm or dual-arm machine?

B2 and M9 are both available in single-arm and dual-arm configurations. The configuration is evaluated during the site survey, based on each site and its needs.

Can a self-service car wash run unattended?

Yes. It can be set up for fully self-service, unattended operation, with drivers operating and paying on their own. Owners can schedule site checks, consumable refills and cleaning as needed.

What is the warranty?

Equipment comes with a 1-year warranty.

How do I get a quote?

Quotes depend on site conditions and equipment configuration. We first arrange a free site survey and then provide a quote.
