01 / OVERVIEW

Product overview

An intelligent control center
for self-service car wash sites.

One control box brings together five wet-zone and seven dry-zone function options. It supports coins, stored-value cards, mobile payments and cloud membership points, with time, amount or all-you-can-use modes configured for your site. A connected cloud backend provides a remote view of operations.

Function options are configured for the site. The nine-key assignment, payments and cloud integration follow the confirmed equipment plan.
