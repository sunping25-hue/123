VEHICLE LENGTH DETECTION TECHNOLOGY

ShinoX Automatic Vehicle Length Detection Technology

Automatically detect vehicle length to control the wash range precisely.

Vehicle lengths vary. From compact cars and standard sedans to SUVs and larger sport utility vehicles, each requires a different wash range.

The ShinoX automatic car wash system uses a vehicle length sensor to detect the length of the vehicle and send that information to the control system, allowing the equipment to carry out the wash according to the vehicle’s length.

How it works

01The vehicle enters the wash areaThe driver follows the instructions to enter the wash area and position the vehicle.
02The system detects the vehicle’s lengthThe sensor detects the vehicle’s length and supplies the information needed to control the equipment.
03The wash follows the vehicle’s lengthBased on the detection results, the car wash controls the corresponding wash travel to clean the vehicle’s body.

Technology features

  • Automatic detectionThere is no need to enter the vehicle’s length manually.
  • Washing based on vehicle lengthThe wash travel is adjusted to suit different vehicle lengths.
  • Less unnecessary washingReduces unnecessary washing beyond the vehicle’s body.
  • Supports different vehicle typesAccommodates different vehicles within the equipment’s permitted dimensions.

ShinoX Intelligent Automatic Car Wash System

By combining vehicle length detection with automated control, the car wash can carry out washing according to each vehicle’s length, improving ease of operation and wash-cycle efficiency.
