Before you build

If you are opening a car wash, first check whether the site can work. This page does not show unauthorized photos or customers.

  • Can the site work?Entrance, driveway, water, power and drainage. We look at this on site.
  • Self-service or touchless?Drivers wash themselves, or an automatic machine does the wash.
  • How should the equipment be set?Bays and machines are decided after a look at the site.
  • PaybackThere is no fixed period, and none is guaranteed.
  • Who looks after it?Even with nobody on site, someone still checks in, restocks and cleans.
