ShinoX Car Wash Systems

Rethinking self-service car wash operations through equipment technology

The car wash industry is changing.

From traditional hand washing to self-service facilities, automated equipment, smart payments and remote operations management, demand for car wash services has moved beyond simply cleaning a vehicle. Customers increasingly seek a more efficient, convenient and flexible washing experience.

ShinoX focuses on car wash equipment, systems integration and complete site development, providing entrepreneurs and car wash operators with comprehensive equipment and site development solutions.

We believe good car wash equipment should be more than hardware. It should form part of a system that fits real operations, meets customer needs and offers long-term maintainability.

From equipment to complete operational solutions

ShinoX services cover self-service car wash equipment, touchless automatic washing systems, supporting cleaning equipment, payment control systems, and site planning and construction integration.

We consider equipment operation, space utilization, ease of maintenance and operational management to help customers select products and system configurations suited to their site conditions, budget and business goals.

For us, an equipment sale is only the beginning of a partnership.

What matters most is how equipment performs after installation: whether it operates reliably, is easy to manage, meets customers’ needs and supports the operator’s continued development.

Our four core principles

01 | Technology Integration

We integrate washing equipment, control systems, payment functions and operational requirements so that different equipment can work effectively together within the same car wash site.

02 | Practical Design

Starting with real usage scenarios, we prioritize ease of operation, site circulation, maintenance space and overall layout. We do not add features at the expense of practical operating needs.

03 | Quality & Service

We emphasize equipment configuration, installation quality, operating guidance and ongoing technical support to help operators reduce uncertainty in equipment management.

04 | Long-term Partnership

From initial consultation and equipment selection to site planning and operation, ShinoX aims to be a partner customers can continue to work with as their car wash business develops.

Equipment for different car wash operating needs

Self-service car wash systems

We provide multifunction self-service wash controls, cleaning systems and supporting equipment integration for self-service car wash sites of different sizes.

Touchless automatic car wash equipment

Automated washing programs bring together high-pressure rinsing, cleaning, care and air drying, giving operators an alternative to traditional hand washing.

Complete car wash site development

We integrate site planning, wash bay layouts, equipment rooms, utilities, canopies and equipment installation to help owners create a complete car wash service environment.

Smart control and payment integration

We introduce payment and management functions appropriate to the equipment solution, making operation more convenient for customers and helping operators understand business performance.

Smarter car wash equipment. More possibilities for your business.

ShinoX aims to do more than provide additional equipment. We continually work to improve the connection between equipment and operations.

Through technology integration, practical design and professional service, we aim to help more entrepreneurs establish their own car wash businesses and give existing operators options for equipment upgrades and operational improvements.
